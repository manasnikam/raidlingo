## Design Context

### Users
Two Twitch streamers setting up a bilingual collaboration under hackathon time pressure, with OBS viewers as the later overlay audience. The phase 2 task is authorizing both broadcasters and a separate bot, checking translation/posting health, and showing a readable merged chat feed in OBS.

### Brand Personality
Chosen working assumption under the user's instruction to make reversible decisions: playful, clear, capable. Use a broadcast control-room feel with warm ink and orange accents.

### Aesthetic Direction
Compact, readable console with oversized seat labels, an editorial title, strong status text, and a chronological event monitor. Preact, TypeScript, and CSS; no decorative assets needed for setup. Overlay must remain transparent at 1920×1080.

### Design Principles
- Connection truth before decoration; never imply a disconnected service is live.
- One obvious authorization action per streamer.
- Show actionable errors inline, without exposing credentials.
- Support keyboard navigation, small screens, and high-contrast text.
- Chat translation is implemented in phase 2; keep voice clearly marked as a future phase.
- Viewer overlays prioritize large translated text, quieter originals, and visible source attribution; diagnostic details belong on the control page.
